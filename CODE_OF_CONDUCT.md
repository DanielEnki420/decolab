# Code of Conduct

Decolab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient, also with beginners. Everyone was one once.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or personal attacks, and no
  publishing of other people's private information.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you were wrong.

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions,
and to anyone who represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub: open a
[private report](https://github.com/DanielEnki420/decolab/security/advisories/new)
and start the title with "Conduct". Reports are read by the maintainer only
and handled confidentially. You get a first answer within 14 days.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious it is: a private note, a public warning, a
temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces, as described in
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
